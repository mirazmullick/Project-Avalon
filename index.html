OTA share of the total travel market today, the gap Avalon is built to close

The market expands at nearly 15 percent a year and the shift online is only starting. Most travel spend in Bangladesh still isn't booked through an OTA at all. Avalon doesn't need to take share from GoZayaan or ShareTrip to grow, it needs to convert the far larger pool that hasn't gone digital, starting with the agency's own B2B relationships.

The audience to reach it already exists. Bangladesh has over 82 million internet users and 64 million on social media. TikTok ads reach 47.9 percent of adults and 67.9 percent of the internet user base. LinkedIn reaches under 8 percent, so it is used narrowly rather than as the main B2B channel.

05

One brand, three audiences, one system

Avalon carries a 35-year reputation online for the first time. That reputation is the asset, and the brand system exists to protect it while it stretches across three very different audiences.

One identity, applied everywhere

The same visual identity, palette, and wordmark appear on every B2B deck, every B2C social post, and every B2E pitch document. No touchpoint should look like a different company than the one an agent already trusts.

Same voice, calibrated by audience

B2B is direct and data-led, travel managers want proof not persuasion. B2C is warm and story-led, a legacy agency going digital. B2E is outcome-focused, built on one signed pilot rather than broad claims. The words change, the brand underneath does not.

A single point of ownership

Brand and digital sign-off sits with one owner rather than split across teams. Every public-facing piece is checked against one style guide before it ships, weekly for paid social, monthly for case studies and PR.

Protecting inherited trust

Every message is measured against one test: does this match how the legacy agency would show up in person. Complaints get a same-day response protocol, because early trust online compounds or breaks fast in this market.

Coordinated with the agency floor

B2B messaging goes through the agency's existing relationship managers, not sent blind. What a sub-agent hears from marketing matches what they hear from the contact they already know.

06

Visibility, awareness, word of mouth

Each segment moves through the same three stages, but the tactic underneath differs every time, because a corporate travel manager, a retail traveller, and an HR head don't discover trust the same way.

B2B

Brand visibility

Agent portal presence inside the parent agency's existing network. One identity across every deck, portal screen, and one-pager agents see.

Awareness

Meta and TikTok ads to agency owners and SME decision makers, LinkedIn held back for corporate travel managers. Monthly webinars, agent meet sessions in Dhaka and Chattogram, and counter visits through the agency's existing relationship managers.

Word-of-mouth conversion

A referral commission for agents who bring in other agents, backed by gifting and recognition for top producers. Agents talk to agents daily, this turns that conversation into acquisition.

B2C

Brand visibility

A teaser landing page live from month one, waitlist only. Light, consistent content so the brand feels familiar by the time it launches.

Awareness

Creator seeding across Meta and TikTok built on story, a legacy agency going digital, not product features. Two PR pushes: announcement, then launch.

Word-of-mouth conversion

A referral-gated waitlist, refer three friends to move up. Curiosity becomes unpaid distribution instead of paid reach.

B2E

Brand visibility

The same identity, translated into a corporate one-pager and a case study document once B2B has real wins to show.

Awareness

Direct outreach to 15 to 20 target companies, HR and admin heads, not marketing. Sales-led, minimal media spend.

Word-of-mouth conversion

One signed pilot becomes the reference case for the next prospect. Peer companies refer once there is a result to point to.

08

Where this goes past month seven

Year one

The digital arm of a trusted name

B2B revenue funds the operation while the B2C waitlist and word of mouth compound quietly. By launch, Avalon isn't a new name asking for trust, it's the digital front door of an agency people already trust, with a waitlist that grew itself.

Year two

B2C scales on referral, not reach

The referral mechanics built in month one carry into a real growth loop. Paid acquisition supplements word of mouth instead of replacing it, keeping acquisition cost low against OTAs that built their base on ad spend alone.

Year three

B2E becomes a second engine

A portfolio of signed corporate pilots turns B2E from a pilot program into its own sales motion, with the agency's reputation and Avalon's platform indistinguishable in the market.
